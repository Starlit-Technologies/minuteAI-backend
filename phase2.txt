1.  PLATFORM VISION & POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Most meeting tools are passive — they sit in the corner, record what happens,
and hand you a transcript afterwards. Steph is fundamentally different. The
platform is designed to be an active, voice-driven intelligence layer that
operates before, during, and after every meeting — serving both individual
contributors and business leaders across all industries.

The platform serves a wide range of users: sales teams closing deals, developers
running standups, executives making strategic decisions, and professionals
preparing for high-stakes presentations. What unites them is a shared need — to
make every meeting count and never lose a critical piece of context again.

  "Most platforms give you records of the past.
   Steph gives you leverage over what happens next."

THE THREE PILLARS OF STEPH:

  Pillar 1 — MEMORY
  Steph remembers everything across all meetings, documents, and commitments.
  User Benefit: Nothing falls through the cracks — ever.

  Pillar 2 — INTELLIGENCE
  Steph synthesises information and surfaces insights proactively.
  User Benefit: Leaders always walk in prepared.

  Pillar 3 — PRESENCE
  Steph participates live — listening, speaking, and responding on command.
  User Benefit: Every meeting has an expert participant with perfect recall.

2.  TECHNOLOGY STACK — DEEPGRAM & ASSEMBLYAI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The platform is built on two of the most powerful speech-to-text engines
available today — Deepgram and AssemblyAI. Rather than choosing one, the
architecture leverages both strategically, routing audio through whichever
engine best fits the use case. This gives Steph best-in-class accuracy, speed,
and intelligence across all meeting types.

── DEEPGRAM — Real-Time Streaming STT ──────────────────────────────────────────

Deepgram is optimised for ultra-low latency, real-time transcription. It is the
engine that powers Steph's live listening during calls. Its Nova-2 model achieves
word error rates competitive with human transcription and processes audio in near
real-time — critical for Steph to detect her wake word and respond promptly
without awkward delays.

Deepgram strengths leveraged in Steph:
  • Streaming transcription via WebSocket — Steph hears the meeting as it
    happens, word by word
  • Speaker diarisation — identifies who is speaking at any moment, enabling
    per-person action item attribution
  • Custom vocabulary — train on industry terms, product names, and internal
    jargon for accurate transcription
  • Wake word detection — Deepgram's streaming API detects 'Steph' (or the
    user's custom bot name) in real time
  • Punctuation and formatting — produces clean, readable transcripts without
    manual cleanup
  • Language detection — automatically identifies and switches between supported
    languages mid-conversation

── ASSEMBLYAI — Post-Processing & Deep Intelligence ────────────────────────────

AssemblyAI is the platform's post-meeting intelligence engine. Where Deepgram
excels at speed, AssemblyAI excels at understanding. After each meeting, the
transcript is passed through AssemblyAI's LeMUR and Audio Intelligence models to
extract meaning, generate summaries, and identify key moments — fuelling the AI
Chief of Staff layer.

AssemblyAI capabilities used:
  • LeMUR (Large Language Model for Universal Retrieval) — ask natural language
    questions over any transcript
  • Auto Chapters — automatically segments the meeting into titled topics with
    timestamps
  • Action Items extraction — identifies commitments, owners, and deadlines from
    natural conversation
  • Sentiment Analysis — detects emotional tone per speaker, per sentence,
    fuelling the Deal Sentiment Tracker
  • PII redaction — automatically removes personal identifiable information from
    sensitive meeting transcripts
  • Content Safety Detection — flags inappropriate or legally sensitive language
    in recorded calls
  • Key Phrases — extracts the most important concepts from a meeting for
    flashcard and summary generation
  • Speaker labels — cross-referenced with Deepgram diarisation for highly
    accurate attribution

── HOW THE TWO ENGINES WORK TOGETHER ───────────────────────────────────────────

  Stage                  | Engine         | What Happens
  -----------------------|----------------|------------------------------------------
  Live Call — Real Time  | Deepgram       | Streams audio, detects wake word, transcribes
  Live Call — Response   | Claude LLM     | Processes Steph's query, generates response
  Post-Meeting Pass 1    | AssemblyAI     | Deep NLP: summaries, action items, sentiment
  Post-Meeting Pass 2    | Claude LLM     | Chief of Staff: patterns, risks, briefing
  Storage                | Platform DB    | Transcript, summary, embeddings per thread
  Retrieval              | Vector Search  | Semantic search across all meeting content

  !! IMPORTANT NOTE !!
  Deepgram streams audio via WebSocket during live calls. AssemblyAI processes
  the final recording post-call. Never run both simultaneously on the same audio
  stream — this doubles cost without benefit. Route live audio to Deepgram only,
  then send the completed recording to AssemblyAI.

3.  PDF TO AUDIO — THE ENTRY FEATURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PDF to Audio is the platform's lowest-friction entry point — the feature that
gets users in the door. A professional uploads a report, proposal, or
presentation and receives a polished, narrated audio version they can listen to
while commuting, exercising, or preparing for a high-stakes meeting.

Core enhancements beyond basic narration:

  ▶ Summary Layering
  Users choose between three listening modes: full audio narration, a 5-minute
  intelligent summary that preserves all key decisions, or a 60-second headline
  brief. The summary is generated by Claude after AssemblyAI extracts key phrases
  from the document text.

  ▶ Voice & Style Selection
  Choose from multiple narrator personas — a professional boardroom voice, a
  conversational peer tone, or a neutral academic style. Powered by ElevenLabs
  TTS for high-quality, natural-sounding output across languages and accents.

  ▶ Slide-to-Script Generator
  Converts a PowerPoint or PDF slide deck into a full presenter script —
  paragraph by paragraph, slide by slide. Ideal for executives preparing for
  board presentations or professionals rehearsing client pitches. The script is
  also narrated as audio.

  ▶ Comprehension Quiz
  After listening, users are offered a short quiz generated by Claude based on
  the document content. Questions are multiple choice and short answer. Results
  are stored on the dashboard to track knowledge retention over time.

  ▶ Smart Flashcard Generator
  AssemblyAI's Key Phrases model extracts the most important concepts from the
  document. Claude then converts these into spaced-repetition flashcards
  presented in the app — ideal for preparing for technical presentations or
  complex client meetings.

  ▶ Multilingual Narration
  Upload a PDF in any language and receive the audio in the same language, or
  choose to translate and narrate it in a different language — useful for
  multinational teams preparing localised presentations.

4.  MEETING INTELLIGENCE FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ▶ Action Item Tracker
  Using AssemblyAI's Action Items model, the platform automatically extracts
  every commitment made during a meeting — including who made it and any deadline
  mentioned. Items are assigned to participants by name, appear on a shared
  dashboard, and send reminders as deadlines approach.
    • Automatic attribution by speaker using diarisation
    • Deadline detection from natural language — 'by end of week', 'before Thursday'
    • Status tracking — open, in progress, completed, overdue
    • Weekly digest email showing all outstanding items across all meetings

  ▶ Meeting ROI Calculator
  Every meeting has a real financial cost that most teams never see. Steph
  estimates this automatically based on attendee roles, number of people on the
  call, and meeting duration. The ROI is displayed on the meeting summary page.
    • Cost-per-minute displayed live during the meeting
    • Monthly meeting cost report per team
    • Suggestions for converting recurring meetings to async updates

  ▶ Silent Participant Detector
  Deepgram's speaker diarisation reveals who speaks and who does not. Steph
  tracks this over time and surfaces engagement insights to team leads.
    • Per-person speaking time chart visible on the dashboard
    • Trend alerts: 'Sarah has not spoken in the last 4 team meetings'
    • Suggested actions: restructure invite list or assign speaking roles

  ▶ Agenda Enforcer
  Before the meeting, the host uploads or dictates an agenda. Steph tracks how
  long each item takes in real time and alerts the host when a topic is running
  over. At the end, a time audit shows how the meeting's duration was spent.
    • Live topic timer visible only to the host
    • Automatic topic detection from conversation content
    • 'Steph, we're moving on' command to log time and advance to the next item

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    • Alert: 'Client tone has dropped significantly over the last 3 interactions'

  ▶ Follow-Up Email Drafter

  ▶ Competitive Intelligence Listener
  When a competitor's name is mentioned during a sales call, Steph flags it and

  come up most often and in what context.
    • Competitor mention frequency dashboard

9.  AI CHIEF OF STAFF — THE CORE VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The AI Chief of Staff is not a feature — it is the product's central identity.
Every other capability feeds into it. The Chief of Staff layer sits across all
of a user's meetings, documents, emails, and calendar, and behaves like a senior
executive assistant with perfect memory and always-on availability.

Critically, the Chief of Staff does not wait to be asked. It is proactive by
design. It surfaces the right information at the right moment — before a meeting,
during a call, or in a morning briefing — without the user having to dig for it.

  ▶ Pre-Meeting Briefings
  15 minutes before any calendar meeting, Steph delivers a personalised brief:
  who is attending, what was discussed in previous meetings with these people,
  any open action items from the last session, relevant documents uploaded by
  attendees, and suggested talking points.

  ▶ Commitment Tracking Across All Meetings
  Every promise made in any meeting is tracked — regardless of which meeting it
  came from. Both appear in the user's personal commitment dashboard with
  deadlines and status. Crucially, CEO meeting commitments and sales meeting
  commitments are tracked in separate, isolated threads.

  ▶ Pattern Detection
  Over weeks and months, the platform identifies patterns that no human would
  notice manually: a decision deferred four times, a vendor repeatedly mentioned
  but never actioned, a recurring conflict in every planning meeting.

  ▶ Daily Intelligence Briefing
  Each morning, Steph delivers a 2-minute audio or text briefing: today's
  meetings and what you need to know for each, overdue action items, risks flagged
  from yesterday's meetings, and one strategic observation from the past week.

  ▶ Difficult Conversation Preparation
  Based on historical sentiment data, Steph identifies when a client relationship
  is deteriorating. Before the next call, it briefs the host on what changed,
  what the likely concern is, and how to approach the conversation.

10. MEETING CONTEXT ISOLATION — CRITICAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is one of the most important design decisions in the entire platform. A user
will have multiple recurring meetings of entirely different natures — a weekly
sales review, a developer standup, a CEO check-in, a client call, a board
preparation session. Each of these is a separate context with separate history,
participants, and sensitive information.

  !! CRITICAL WARNING !!
  If Steph ever references information from the wrong meeting — mentioning a
  sales pipeline number during a CEO meeting, or a hiring discussion during a
  client call — the host loses trust in the platform immediately. Context
  contamination is a trust-destroying bug, not just a minor error. This must
  be architected correctly from day one.

HOW MEETING CONTEXT ISOLATION WORKS:

Every meeting is assigned to a Meeting Thread — a persistent, named context that
groups all sessions involving the same recurring group of people. Steph builds
and maintains each thread independently, and never crosses threads unless the
host explicitly requests it.

Thread Identification — How Steph Knows Which Thread It Is In:
  • Participant fingerprint — the unique combination of attendees is the primary
    identifier
  • Calendar event link — the event ID anchors the thread if calendar-integrated
  • Recurring pattern — same participants, same day/time are auto-grouped
  • Topic fingerprinting — AssemblyAI's Auto Chapters verify meeting type if
    the participant list is ambiguous
  • Host confirmation — on first detection, Steph asks the host to confirm and
    name the thread (e.g. 'Sales Weekly')

Thread Memory Rules:
  Rule                  | Behaviour
  ----------------------|------------------------------------------------------
  Default scope         | Steph only recalls history from the current thread
  Cross-thread recall   | Only triggered when host explicitly asks for it
  Permission control    | Host can lock threads — preventing cross-referencing
  New participant joins | Steph does not volunteer historical context to new attendees
  Guest attendees       | External guests trigger restricted mode — no internal history

  SCENARIO — CEO Meeting in Progress:
  The host is in a CEO check-in. Earlier that day they had a sales meeting where
  the team discussed a sensitive deal falling apart. The CEO asks about Q2
  pipeline health. Steph does NOT volunteer information from the sales meeting.
  She responds only from data the CEO meeting thread has. The host can say
  'Steph, also check the sales thread' to explicitly authorise the cross-reference.

Meeting Thread Dashboard — Example:
  Thread Name       | Participants        | Sessions | Open Actions | Status
  ------------------|---------------------|----------|--------------|------------------
  Sales Weekly      | Sales Team (6)      | 24       | 3            | Active
  Dev Standup       | Engineering (8)     | 48       | 7            | Active
  CEO Check-in      | CEO + You           | 12       | 1            | Active — Locked
  Client: Acme Corp | External + You      | 6        | 2            | Active — Restricted
  Board Prep Q2     | Exec Team (4)       | 3        | 5            | Active — Locked

11. PREVIOUS MEETING CONTINUITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When Steph detects that a meeting belongs to a known thread, she automatically
prepares a 'Last Time' briefing — a concise recap of what happened in the
previous session of that specific thread. The host can trigger this briefing to
be read aloud to the room at the start of the meeting, creating instant
accountability with no effort.

What the Continuity Briefing Contains:
  • Summary of the last session's key decisions — in plain, spoken language
  • Action items from last time — listed by owner with status
  • Deferred items — topics that were raised but explicitly postponed
  • Attendance note — flagging if someone new has joined or a regular is absent
  • Time since last meeting — 'This is your 8th session, 7 days since your last.'

  SCENARIO — Sales Weekly Opening:
  Host presses 'Read Last Minutes' on the control panel. Steph says:
  'Good morning. Welcome to your 24th Sales Weekly. Last Tuesday, the team
  discussed three main topics: the Acme Corp proposal, the Q2 target gap, and
  the new CRM rollout timeline. Three action items were raised. Marcus was to
  send the revised proposal to Acme by Friday — that has been marked complete.
  Priya was to pull the Q2 forecast numbers — still open. The CRM timeline
  decision was deferred to today. You have 45 minutes scheduled. Shall we begin?'

Thread Timeline View:
Beyond individual session continuity, the platform provides a full Thread
Timeline — a visual history of every session in a meeting thread, showing how
topics, decisions, and team dynamics have evolved over weeks and months. Leaders
can scroll back through a year of CEO check-ins and see exactly when strategic
priorities shifted.

12. STEPH — IDENTITY, VOICE & PERSONALISATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Steph is not a generic assistant — she is a named, voiced, personalised team
member. The default name is Steph, but every user can rename their bot from the
dashboard. When renamed, the bot responds exclusively to the new name — both as
a voice command trigger and as how she introduces herself when joining a call.

  "Teams will start saying 'let's ask Steph' naturally in meetings.
   That familiarity is the product's best retention mechanism.
   Switching platforms means losing Steph's memory — and that is a
   loss no team wants to take."

Dashboard Personalisation Settings:
  Setting           | Default        | Options
  ------------------|----------------|------------------------------------------
  Bot Name          | Steph          | Any name — Max, Nova, Aria, Alex, custom
  Voice Gender      | Female         | Female / Male / Neutral
  Voice Style       | Professional   | Professional / Friendly / Academic / Concise
  Personality       | Balanced       | Slider: Direct & Concise <-> Warm & Conversational
  Response Length   | Medium         | Brief (1-2 sentences) / Medium / Detailed
  Language          | English        | Multilingual — 30+ languages via Deepgram
  Trigger Access    | Host Only      | Host Only / All Participants / Custom
  Join Behaviour    | Silent         | Silent until called / Intro announcement on join
  Call Platform     | All            | Zoom / Google Meet / Teams / Phone / Custom

When a user renames their bot, all voice commands, calendar integrations, and
notification messages update automatically to use the new name.

13. VOICE ACTIVATION & WAKE WORD INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Steph listens passively using Deepgram's real-time streaming API throughout every
call. She remains completely silent until her name is spoken — at which point she
activates, processes the request, and responds within 1-2 seconds.

Wake Word Detection Logic:
  • Deepgram streams audio continuously via WebSocket during the call
  • A lightweight pattern matcher scans the transcript stream for the bot's name
  • Detection confidence threshold is set high to avoid false positives
  • Two-second confirmation window — if no follow-up command is detected, Steph
    does not activate
  • Name collision handling — if someone on the call shares the bot's name, the
    system uses context and intonation to distinguish a command from a reference
  • The host can also trigger Steph via the silent control panel without speaking,
    giving full fallback control

Activation Flow — Step by Step:
  Step | What Happens                                          | Technology
  -----|-------------------------------------------------------|----------------------
  1    | Deepgram streams audio from the call in real time    | Deepgram WebSocket STT
  2    | Bot name is identified in the transcript stream      | Pattern match on tokens
  3    | System waits up to 2 seconds for a command to follow | Buffer logic
  4    | Full command is transcribed and parsed               | Deepgram + NLP
  5    | Claude processes the command against memory/context  | Claude API
  6    | A natural spoken response is generated               | ElevenLabs TTS
  7    | Response plays through Steph's audio stream          | WebRTC audio injection
  8    | Steph returns to silent listening mode               | Deepgram resumes stream

14. REAL-TIME VOICE INTERACTION MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When activated, Steph can operate in several distinct modes depending on what
the host needs. Each mode is accessible via voice command or the control panel.

  ▶ Public Mode — Speaks to the Room
  Steph speaks aloud and all participants hear her. Used for reading minutes,
  answering factual questions, or delivering a contribution to the full group.
  This is the default mode when the host triggers Steph.
    • Reading last meeting minutes to open the session
    • Answering a factual question raised by any participant
    • Delivering a data point or document reference on request
    • Time-keeping announcements — 'You have 5 minutes left on this topic'

  ▶ Silent Co-Pilot Mode — Speaks Only to the Host
  Steph's audio is routed only to the host through a private earpiece or headphone
  channel. The rest of the room hears nothing. This allows the host to receive
  real-time coaching and warnings during a high-stakes conversation.
    • Sentiment warning: 'The client's tone has shifted — consider pausing'
    • Negotiation cue: 'This is the third time price has come up'
    • Fact retrieval: 'The figure you're looking for is $2.4M — from the March deck'
    • Time warning: 'You have 8 minutes left and contract terms haven't been discussed'

  ▶ Devil's Advocate Mode
  The host sets Steph to challenge whatever proposal is on the table. Steph
  presents the strongest counter-argument to the current idea — helping the team
  stress-test decisions before committing.
    • Host activates via control panel before or during discussion
    • Steph's challenge is based on data from past meetings and LLM reasoning
    • 'Steph, challenge this plan' triggers an immediate structured counter-argument

  ▶ Neutral Mediator Mode
  When two participants disagree and the discussion becomes circular, the host
  invokes Steph as a neutral party. She synthesises both positions, identifies
  the core point of disagreement, and presents an objective perspective.
    • Summarises both sides of the disagreement without bias
    • Identifies the specific factual question that would resolve the disagreement
    • Suggests a decision framework if the data is ambiguous

15. HOST CONTROL PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The host has exclusive access to a real-time control panel — a floating sidebar
on desktop or a companion mobile app that only they can see. This is the command
centre for Steph during a live meeting.

Control Panel Capabilities:
  • One-tap trigger for any Steph action — no need to speak aloud
  • Interrupt button — stops Steph mid-sentence immediately if untimely
  • Mode switcher — toggle between Public, Silent Co-Pilot, Devil's Advocate,
    Mediator
  • Timed queue — schedule an announcement for Steph to deliver at a set time
  • Private question field — type a question; choose ear-only or aloud
  • Participant permissions toggle — grant or revoke other participants' ability
    to call Steph
  • Live transcript view — see the rolling transcript in real time
  • Action item flag — tap to mark any spoken statement as an action item
  • Thread selector — confirm or switch which Meeting Thread this session belongs
  • End meeting button — triggers Steph to wrap up and send the summary

Full Voice Command Reference:
  Voice Command                        | Steph's Action                          | Audience
  -------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|----------
  "[Name], read last minutes"          | Reads previous session summary aloud     | Room
  "[Name], what was decided about X?"  | Retrieves specific decision from history  | Room
  "[Name], who owns this action?"      | Attributes ownership from context         | Room
  "[Name], what do you think?"         | Gives data-backed contribution            | Room
  "[Name], challenge this"             | Activates Devil's Advocate mode           | Room
  "[Name], how long on this topic?"    | Reports time elapsed on current item      | Room
  "[Name], take a note"                | Logs the following words as a key note    | Internal
  "[Name], go silent"                  | Mutes until explicitly called again       | Internal
  "[Name], switch to co-pilot"         | Activates Silent Co-Pilot mode            | Host only
  "[Name], summarise so far"           | Summarises meeting to current point       | Room
  "[Name], end the meeting"            | Wraps up and sends summary to all         | Room

16. STEPH AS AN INTELLIGENT CONTRIBUTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Beyond commands and memory retrieval, Steph can function as a genuine
intellectual contributor to the conversation. When a complex question is raised
and the host invites her opinion, Steph synthesises information from multiple
sources to give a structured, useful response in real time.

Sources Steph draws from when contributing:
  Source                  | What Steph Retrieves                               | Scope
  ------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|-------------------
  Meeting Thread History  | Decisions, context, commitments from past sessions | Current thread only
  Uploaded Documents      | Data, figures, policies from thread files          | Thread-specific
  Cross-Thread (request)  | Context from other threads when host authorises    | Explicit request
  LLM Reasoning           | Synthesised analysis, frameworks, opinions         | Always available
  Web Search (optional)   | Current market data, news, benchmarks              | When enabled

  SCENARIO — Business Expansion Question:
  A startup team is debating whether to expand to a new market. Someone asks:
  'Do we actually have the capacity to do this?' The host says: 'Steph, what do
  you think?'

  Steph responds: 'Based on the resource planning document uploaded on March 4th
  and the hiring timeline discussed in your last Dev Standup, the team would be
  understaffed by approximately 3 roles to execute this expansion before Q3.
  Marcus raised this capacity concern in the February 18th planning session but
  it was deferred. The current open roles in the hiring tracker have an estimated
  8-week fill time, which puts first hires in late Q2 at the earliest. Would you
  like me to outline what a delayed expansion timeline would look like?'

This response demonstrates the platform's true value: not just retrieving a fact,
but connecting data across documents, past meetings, and logical reasoning — all
in under 3 seconds.

19. MONETISATION CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The platform's feature depth supports a tiered SaaS model with clear upgrade
incentives at each level.

  Tier          | Price Signal | Key Features Included
  --------------|--------------|------------------------------------------------------
  Starter       | Free / Low   | PDF to Audio, basic transcription, 1 thread, 5hr/month
  Professional  | Mid          | AI Chief of Staff, unlimited threads, context isolation
  Team          | Per seat     | Full Steph bot, host control panel, sentiment tracking
  Enterprise    | Custom       | Custom bot per dept, API access, SSO, compliance, Steph API

Key monetisation notes:
  • Deepgram and AssemblyAI have usage-based costs — pass through a margin on
    audio processing hours at higher tiers
  • ElevenLabs TTS has character limits at lower pricing tiers — factor into
    PDF to Audio limits per plan
  • The live Steph bot (WebRTC + real-time STT + TTS) is the most expensive
    feature to run — reserve for Team tier and above
  • Thread storage and vector search costs scale with usage — archive old threads
    after 12 months at Starter tier
  • Enterprise clients will pay significant premiums for the Steph API to embed
    the bot in their own internal tools

  "The product's greatest commercial asset is switching cost. Once a team has
   6 months of meeting thread history, action item records, and institutional
   memory stored in Steph — leaving means starting over.
   Build for depth from day one."
